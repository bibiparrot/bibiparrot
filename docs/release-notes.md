BibiParrot turns your own audio and video into a listening and dictation workspace.

- Replay a sentence or a single word, loop difficult passages, and slow down playback.
- Write formatted study notes and link any text directly to the audio it refers to.
- Use offline speech recognition with fast volume-based segmentation and bounded parallel recognition.
- Arrange the media player, library, segment tree, notes and practice assistant to suit your workflow.
- Choose from eight interface languages.

All packages include the offline speech models and media tools. Downloads cover Windows x86_64, macOS Apple Silicon and Intel, and Linux x86_64 and aarch64. Check SHA256SUMS.txt to verify your download.

macOS packages are ad-hoc signed, without Apple notarization. Linux builds target Ubuntu 24.04 or newer and distributions with glibc 2.39 or newer.
